## Qualification rubric

- **Fit:** local service business with an active Google Maps profile, public phone number and no Website button on Google Maps.
- **Signals:** active hours, useful review volume, clear service area and a service where trust/urgent enquiries matter.
- **Priority:** plumbing, electrical, roofing, aircond, renovation or other home services with enough project value to support a RM3,500 website.
- **Disqualify:** dormant listings, businesses that already have a Google Maps Website button, or listings with no clear business identity.
